Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 23, 2026

AISocialsManager stores your data only on your own computer. Marketo Cat does not hold copies of your social media data on any server, so you control deletion directly.

1. Disconnect an account in the app

  1. Open AISocialsManager and go to the brand's Channels settings.
  2. Choose the connected account (for example, your Facebook Page or LinkedIn profile) and select Disconnect.
  3. The app deletes that account's access token, refresh token and stored profile and page details.

2. Remove the app's access on the platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites > find AISocialsManager > Remove. When you remove the app, Meta stops sharing data with it.
  • LinkedIn: Settings > Data privacy > Permitted services > AISocialsManager > Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions > AISocialsManager > Remove access.
  • X: Settings > Security and account access > Apps and sessions > Connected apps > AISocialsManager > Revoke.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions > AISocialsManager > Remove access.

3. Delete everything

To delete all data, including posts, media and schedules, uninstall the app and delete its data folder on your computer.

Need help or want confirmation?

Email hello@marketocat.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will reply within 30 days, confirm that we hold no copy of your platform data on our systems, and help you delete it from your device. Any email correspondence we hold about you will also be deleted on request.
