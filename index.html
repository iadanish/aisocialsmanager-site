How it works

From draft to published in three steps

1

Connect your accounts

Sign in through each network’s official login and choose exactly which pages, channels or profiles Marketocat may post to.

2

Write and schedule

Draft a post, attach images or video, tailor it per network, and pick a date and time on the calendar.

3

Marketocat publishes

At the scheduled time the post goes out through the official API, and you get a link to the live post.

Features

Built for people who run more than one brand

Multi-brand workspaces

Keep each brand’s accounts, drafts, media and calendar separate, and switch between them in one click.

One post, every network

Write once, then tailor the caption, hashtags and media for each network before it goes out.

Visual calendar

See every scheduled post by day and week. Drag to reschedule, or cancel before it publishes.

Local-first by design

Content, media and account tokens stay on your computer. No Marketocat server ever sees them.

Official APIs only

Accounts connect through each platform’s own sign-in (OAuth), and every post uses its official publishing API.

Clear publish status

See whether each post was published, with a direct link to it, or why a platform rejected it.

Privacy

Your data stays on your computer

Marketocat stores posts, media and access tokens locally. It talks only to the networks you connect, only to do what you scheduled. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Where is my data stored?+

On your own computer. Marketocat is local-first: posts, media, schedules and access tokens live in a local database. We do not run a server that receives them.

Which networks can I publish to?+

LinkedIn (personal profiles and company pages), Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, X, YouTube and TikTok.

Does Marketocat post anything on its own?+

No. It only publishes posts you wrote and scheduled, to the accounts you picked. It never likes, follows, comments or sends messages.

How do I disconnect an account or delete my data?+

Disconnect the account in Marketocat to delete its tokens, and revoke access in the platform’s settings. Full steps are on our data deletion page.
