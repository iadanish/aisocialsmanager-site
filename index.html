Plan and publish social posts for every brand you manage

AISocialsManager is a local-first social media scheduler made by Marketo Cat. Write a post once, adapt it for each network, schedule it, and let the app publish it to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok.

Multiple brands

Keep each brand's accounts, drafts, media and calendar separate in one workspace.

Six networks

Publish to LinkedIn personal profiles and company pages, Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, X, YouTube and TikTok.

Local-first

Your content, media and account tokens are stored on your own computer, not on our servers.

Scheduling calendar

See what goes out and when, then reschedule or cancel before it publishes.

How it works

  1. Connect your social accounts with each platform's official sign-in (OAuth). You choose which pages or accounts the app can use.
  2. Write a post, attach images or video, and adjust the text for each network.
  3. Pick a date and time. At that time the app publishes the post through the platform's official API.
  4. Disconnect any account at any time. That deletes its access token from your computer.

What the app does with your social accounts

AISocialsManager only publishes content you created and scheduled, and reads the basic profile and page information needed to show which account you are posting to. It does not post on its own, follow or message people, or collect data about your audience. See the Privacy Policy for details.
